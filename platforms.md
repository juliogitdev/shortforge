# Descriptions per platform (always delivered with the video)

Write all of them into `captions` in plan.json. The build writes `out/descricoes.md`. Also paste them in chat for the user.

| Platform | Shape | Limits and tips |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | 1 or 2 short lines: hook + question + 4 to 6 hashtags | Caption is about 2,200 chars, but short wins. Put the question first. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Hook line, 2 or 3 context lines, the A/B or the question, a teaser, then 6 to 9 hashtags at the end | Line breaks help. Emojis are OK. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | **Title** (at most about 70 chars, curiosity + #shorts) + description (2 to 4 lines + 3 hashtags) | The title matters most. Don't put an unproven allegation as fact in the title. |
| **Kwai** | 1 punchy line + question + 4 or 5 hashtags | Casual tone. |
| **Facebook Reels** | 2 to 4 plain lines + question + 3 hashtags | Older audience, so spell things out. |
| **Pinned comment** | One line, the same everywhere: "🗳️ VOTAÇÃO: comenta A ou B 👇 ..." | Pin it right after posting. |

Hashtag recipe: 1 or 2 broad tags (#historia, #games) + 2 niche tags (#gta6, #vocedecide) + the channel tag (#zereihoje or the user's handle). Avoid banned or spammy tags and mass-tag lists.

Tips to include in `captions.tips`:
- the cover frame (the most shocking 3D frame, with its time)
- posting time (the channel's fixed slots, for example 12h and 18h)
- reply to the first comments within the first hour
- for TikTok and Instagram, the user may add the licensed trending song in the app at about 15 to 20% volume
